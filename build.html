Turn a Spotify playlist into the set of records Linework draws from. You log in once here; visitors to the app never need to.

1 Connect Spotify

Use your app from the Spotify Developer Dashboard. Add this page’s address as a Redirect URI:

2 Choose a playlist

It must be a playlist you own or collaborate on. Spotify only lets development-mode apps read those. To use someone else’s, open it in Spotify, select all tracks and add them to a new playlist of yours.

3 Review the tags

Linework matches a drawing’s motif (found in titles) and its linework (set by genre). Fix anything that looks off; your changes are saved in this browser as you go.

    4 Publish

    Try your catalog in the app first, then download catalog.json, put it next to index.html in your repo and push. The app switches to catalog mode automatically: no login for anyone.
